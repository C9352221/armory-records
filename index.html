About

Boutique health & longevity for clients who value discretion.

Armory Records Inc is a boutique health, wellness, consulting and fitness company that customizes multiple methodologies to facilitate optimum health and longevity for higher-end affluent clients — athletes, musicians, actors, and other professionals.

We offer personalized diets, fitness, supplements and more, primarily at our clients' homes or places of business. Over the past decade, word of mouth and discretion have been central to our growth and sustainability. Most of our clients prefer to remain out of the spotlight. We intend to keep it that way.

— Matthew Messina, President
